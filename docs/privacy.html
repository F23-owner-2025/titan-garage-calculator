Calculator privacy & contact

Effective September 15, 2026. This notice describes this estimate calculator; it does not apply to every Titan service or website.

Information used for your estimate

The estimate is calculated in your browser. When you select “Send my estimate,” the form sends your name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, square footage, garage-size selection, finish choice, estimated total, and calculator website address to Titan through Formspree for estimate follow-up.

The form requests contact about your flooring estimate by email or phone. It is not a signup for automated marketing calls or texts. Viewing an estimate does not require submitting contact information, purchasing a service, or making a payment.

Service providers and technical information

  • Formspree and email providers process and deliver submitted requests. Submissions can be retained in Formspree and in Titan’s email account. See Formspree’s privacy policy for its practices.
  • The hosting service and Cloudflare serve and protect this website. Requests may generate technical records such as IP address, browser information, requested URLs, and access times. Security services may use cookies or similar mechanisms to prevent abuse.
  • UNPKG delivers the Formspree form script. Loading it sends a normal web request, which includes technical connection information, to that provider.

The calculator’s own code does not include advertising or analytics tools and does not save your estimate to browser cookies or local storage. Your browser may separately retain autofill information. Provider practices are separate from the calculator’s in-page calculation.

Retention and your choices

Using “Start over” clears the calculator’s current choices and form fields. Information already submitted is not automatically deleted from Formspree, email accounts, or technical records. Contact Titan to ask about retention or request access, correction, or deletion of submitted information. Do not submit a new estimate solely to make a privacy request.

To make a privacy request, use the email or phone number below and identify this calculator. To ask for review of a response, use the same contact methods and describe it as a privacy appeal. Applicable rights and exceptions depend on the law that applies to your request; identity verification may be needed. Do not email identity documents unless a secure method has been arranged.
